Controls
  • Click empty space — add a vertex
  • Click a vertex — toggle it in the selection; drag — move it (moves the whole selection if it is selected)
  • Shift + drag from one vertex to another — add an edge
  • Drag empty space — box-select vertices (hold Shift to add to the selection)
  • Click an edge, triangle or tetrahedron — select its vertices
  • Enter fill the simplex spanned by the selection (2 vertices → edge, 3 → triangle, 4 → tetrahedron, …), together with all its faces
  • C cone: a new vertex joined to every simplex inside the selection, e.g. to fill a polygonal hole
  • Delete remove the selected simplex and every simplex containing it (its faces stay); Shift+Delete delete the selected vertices
  • Esc clear selection · Ctrl+A select all · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo / redo
  • Wheel zoom · right- or middle-drag pan · F fit view · L auto layout
