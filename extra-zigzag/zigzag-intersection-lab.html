03 / BACKGROUND

What is being computed?

For each k = 0, 1, 2, we compute Hk(K) = ker ∂k / im ∂k+1 over 𝔽₂. The inclusions induce the linear maps Hk(K₀) ← Hk(K₀ ∩ K₁) → Hk(K₁). Write f and g for the maps from the intersection to K₀ and K₁. The decomposition depends on rank(f), rank(g), and rank(f,g), where (f,g) maps into the direct sum. Barcode positions 0, 1, 2 denote K₀, K₀ ∩ K₁, K₁.

Computing H₂ correctly

3-simplices (filled tetrahedra) are supported because classes in dimension 2 can be killed by the image of ∂₃. The drawing is a 2D projection of an abstract complex.
