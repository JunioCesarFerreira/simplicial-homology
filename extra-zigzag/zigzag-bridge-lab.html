03 / MODEL

Connectivity without extra fillings

Start with W, the simplices of C whose vertices all belong to A or B, and every vertex of C. Contract each connected component of W for the purpose of choosing a forest, then add edges of C only when they join different components. The result satisfies A ∪ B ⊆ W ⊆ M ⊆ C, H₀(M) ≅ H₀(C), and Hₖ(W) ≅ Hₖ(M) for k ≥ 1, via the inclusion maps.

Read the construction, proofs, and limitations

A modeling choice, not a union of barcodes

M omits the fillings and cycles that C introduces through vertices outside A and B. It keeps every other simplex of C, so a vertex of A replaced by a nearby vertex of B hands its cycles over. It does not recover cycles already filled in W. Highlighted edges are chosen by vertex ID, not geometric distance. Changing vertex positions does not change the barcode.
