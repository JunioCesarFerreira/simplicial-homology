03 / BACKGROUND

What is being computed?

For each k = 0, 1, 2, we compute Hk(K) = ker ∂k / im ∂k+1 over 𝔽₂. The inclusions induce the linear maps Hk(K₀) → Hk(K₁) ← Hk(K₂). The decomposition depends on the ranks of these maps and the intersection of their images — not just the Betti numbers.

Computing H₂ correctly

3-simplices (filled tetrahedra) are supported because classes in dimension 2 can be killed by the image of ∂₃. The drawing is a 2D projection of an abstract complex.
